ABOUT MY PROJECT CINTERSYS MEDIRECORD

CINTERSYS MEDIRECORD is a secure, offline-first electronic health record (EHR) application designed specifically for non-profit foundations and community healthcare providers. It enables the efficient and confidential management of comprehensive patient clinical histories within a local environment.

Developed with a focus on accessibility and data sovereignty, MEDIRECORD allows medical staff to create, update, and search patient records without requiring a constant internet connection. Its intuitive interface supports key functions such as recording medical history, diagnoses, treatment plans, prescription tracking, and visit notes, all structured to streamline clinical workflows.

By prioritizing on-premise deployment, CINTERSYS MEDIRECORD ensures that sensitive patient data remains under the direct control of the organization, complying with fundamental data protection principles while serving communities with limited or unreliable connectivity. This solution empowers healthcare workers to deliver consistent, informed care through robust and accessible digital record-keeping.
